# One-More-Step-privacyPrivacy
Policy: One More Step: health tracker
Last updated: September 13, 2026

One More Step: health tracker ("the App") is developed and operated by KASMO.

Information We Collect
Personal & Profile Data: Details you provide such as your name, age, weight, height and fitness goals are stored locally on your device only and are never sent to any external server.

Health & Fitness Data: Metrics tracked through app features—including step counts, water intake logs, calorie estimates, alarms, reminders, and achievement badges—are saved locally to power your personal dashboard, analytics charts, and graphs.

Third-Party Ads & Analytics: The App may use services like Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and similar analytics tools may collect an Advertising ID, device info, and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

How We Use Information
Your name, age, and profile details are used only to personalize your fitness and health tracking experience within the App.

Health metrics, step data, water logs, and analytics charts are processed locally to display your daily progress, graphs, and milestone badges.

Advertising and diagnostic data are used to maintain app performance and deliver relevant ads.

Third-Party Services
This App may use Google AdMob for advertisements. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Deletion
You can delete all locally stored data, tracked history, and profile information anytime using the "Delete Data" or "Reset" option in the App's Settings screen, or simply by clearing the app data from your device settings.
